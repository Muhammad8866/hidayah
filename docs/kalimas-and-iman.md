# 6 Kalime and Iman

Open **More → 6 Kalime & Iman**. The screen contains Kalima Tayyibah,
Shahadat, Tamjeed, Tauheed, Istighfar, Radd-e-Kufr, Iman-e-Mujmal and
Iman-e-Mufassal. All text is bundled for offline reading.

Each entry shows Arabic, Roman transliteration and a choice of Roman Urdu
or English explanatory meaning. Search covers all text and ignores Arabic
vowel marks. Large text increases the Arabic font; long-press allows text
selection and copying. Reader choices survive activity recreation.

Traditional wording cross-checked against:

- https://www.islaminperspective.com/topics/fiqh/The-six-Kalima
- https://www.dawateislamiusa.org/pillars_of_islam/shahadah-faith

The meanings are independently phrased. These statements are presented as
a traditional learning collection, without attributing the complete numbered
collection to one Quran verse or hadith.
