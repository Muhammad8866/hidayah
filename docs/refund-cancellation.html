Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Voluntary contributions

Hidayah is free. Any payment is a one-time voluntary contribution to support development, not a purchase of goods, paid features or a subscription.

No refunds after completion

Completed contributions are non-refundable, including payments made by mistake or a change of mind, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Confirm the amount before completing payment.

Cancellation

You can close or abandon checkout before payment completion. A completed contribution cannot be cancelled. There is no recurring subscription to cancel.

Failed, pending or disputed payments

If a payment fails, remains pending or appears to be debited more than once, contact us with its payment reference and date so we can check its status with Razorpay. Never send an OTP, PIN or card password. Bank or payment-provider reversals are separate from this voluntary-contribution refund policy. This policy does not limit rights that applicable law requires us to preserve.
